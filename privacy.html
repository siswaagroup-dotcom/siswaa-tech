Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use tech.siswaa.com or contact Siswaa Technologies.

What this website collects

This website has no database and stores nothing about you. The enquiry form does not send your details to any server. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, it opens WhatsApp on your device with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it.

The site remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts are served from this website, so loading a page does not contact any other company.

The chat assistant

The chat assistant on this site is an AI. When you send it a message, your conversation is sent to Google’s Gemini service to write a reply. Siswaa does not store chat conversations; your browser keeps them only until you close the tab, and the “New chat” button clears them.

  • Google may process and, depending on the service tier, review these messages under its own terms. Please don’t type passwords, OTPs, bank, card, Aadhaar or PAN details into the chat.
  • When the AI is unavailable, the assistant answers from a fixed list of questions inside your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • If you choose “Continue on WhatsApp”, a short summary of your questions is filled into a WhatsApp message, which you can edit before sending.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call us or email us, we receive what you send: usually your name, phone number, business name and what you need. We use it only to reply to you and, if you become a client, to deliver and support your project.

  • We do not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.
  • For client projects, we share only what is needed with the services that run your site, such as domain and hosting providers.
  • You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

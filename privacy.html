Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use tech.siswaa.com or contact Siswaa Technologies.

What this website collects

Nothing is stored by this website. The enquiry form does not send your details to any server. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, it opens WhatsApp on your device with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it.

The site remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address as part of serving them.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call us or email us, we receive what you send: usually your name, phone number, business name and what you need. We use it only to reply to you and, if you become a client, to deliver and support your project.

  • We do not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.
  • For client projects, we share only what is needed with the services that run your site, such as domain and hosting providers.
  • You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

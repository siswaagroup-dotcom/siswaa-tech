Terms

Last updated 14 October 2026

These are the main terms for working with Siswaa Technologies. Each client also receives a short written agreement before work starts, and that agreement is what applies to their project.

Website plans

  • Monthly plans have a one-time setup fee and a 6-month minimum term. After that, you can cancel with 30 days’ notice.
  • Yearly plans are paid upfront for 12 months, with the setup fee waived.
  • Each plan includes the number of updates per month listed on the plans section. Extra work is quoted separately.
  • If a payment is more than 15 days late, the site may be paused until it is paid.

App and custom projects

  • We agree the scope and a fixed price in writing before starting.
  • Payment is in stages as work is delivered, as set out in the project agreement.
  • Changes to the agreed scope get a new quote before we do them.

Your content and domain

Your business name, text, photos and domain are yours. If you leave, we help you move them.

Prices and tax

Prices are in Indian rupees. GST is added where applicable.

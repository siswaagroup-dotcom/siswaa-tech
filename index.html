FAQ

Questions people ask us

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is in your business’s name, and your content and photos are yours. If you ever want to move, we’ll hand everything over.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans have a 6-month minimum, and after that you can cancel whenever you like. Yearly plans run for the year you’ve paid for.

How do I pay?

Monthly plans are paid by UPI AutoPay or card, so you never have to remember. Yearly plans are paid once for the whole year.

How long does it take?

Websites go live in about 7 days once we have your details and photos. For apps and bigger builds, we give you a timeline along with the fixed price.

Do you work outside Delhi?

Yes. We work over WhatsApp, phone and video calls, so it works the same wherever you are in India.

What if I already have a website or app?

We can fix it, update it, redesign it or move it. Start with a free app health check and we’ll tell you, in plain words, what needs work.
